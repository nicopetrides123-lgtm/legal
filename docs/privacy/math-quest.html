Privacy Policy — Math Quest

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Math Quest handles your information. We designed this app to respect your privacy.

The short version

Math Quest does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app runs entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Information we collect

None. We operate no servers and receive no data from you. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or other personal information.

Data stored on your device

Any content you create in the app — such as your progress, settings, entries, or records — is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is removed with it. If your device is set up with iCloud device backups, a copy may be included in your own encrypted Apple backup, which is controlled entirely by you through your Apple account.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects nothing from children. It is safe for users of all ages in that respect.

iCloud sync

Math Quest can sync your progress between your own devices using Apple’s iCloud. That data travels through your Apple account, not through us — we operate no servers and never receive it. Apple handles it under Apple’s privacy policy. You can turn this off in iOS Settings, and deleting the app removes its local copy.

Your choices & rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email nicopetrides123@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “last updated” date.

Contact
