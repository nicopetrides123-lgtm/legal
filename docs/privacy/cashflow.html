Privacy Policy — Cashflow

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cashflow handles your information. We designed this app to respect your privacy.

The short version

Cashflow runs on your device and does not collect personal information. It offers optional in-app purchases that are processed by Apple. There is no advertising and no account.

Information we collect

None directly. We operate no servers and do not receive personal data from you. Your progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

In-app purchases

Cashflow may offer optional purchases (for example, to unlock content). All payments are handled by Apple through the App Store under Apple’s terms. We never see or store your payment card details. Apple provides us only with anonymized information needed to confirm a purchase and to prevent fraud.

Restoring purchases

Purchase records are tied to your Apple Account so you can restore them on your devices. This is managed by Apple, not by us.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases require your Apple Account and any parental controls you have configured.

Your choices & rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email nicopetrides123@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “last updated” date.

Contact
